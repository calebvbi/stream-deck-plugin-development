# Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/calebvbi/stream-deck-plugin-development/issues) for reproducible skill, helper, or documentation defects.

Include:

- the skill version or commit;
- the AI agent and version;
- whether the target is a new or existing plugin;
- relevant Stream Deck, Node.js SDK, CLI, and host versions;
- a minimal sanitized manifest or reproduction;
- the exact command and error;
- the evidence layer that failed: source, package, installed runtime, simulated input, or physical hardware.

Do not post credentials, private paths, customer data, proprietary source, unpublished packages, device identifiers, or generated reports that have not been sanitized. Report security-sensitive problems through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This community project has no guaranteed response time. General third-party API, OAuth, framework, or TypeScript problems are outside scope unless they change Stream Deck behavior.
